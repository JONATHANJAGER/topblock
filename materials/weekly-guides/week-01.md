# Course 1 | Week 1: Understanding cybersecurity and business risk

**Company:** Fictional Apex Global Logistics

**Milestone:** Write a one-page CIA triad and enterprise asset map

### Session 1: Cybersecurity
**Definition:** Protecting digital systems, accounts and information against misuse or disruption
**Everyday example:** A warehouse uses locks and cameras to protect valuable freight
**Business scenario:** At Apex Global Logistics (AGL), an employee mistakenly shares a shipment file with an unauthorized colleague.
**Guided actions:**
1. Write down AGL shipping manifests, employee identities and dispatch service as three assets.
2. Label which CIA property matters most for each specific risk. A record secretly changed affects integrity; a service outage affects availability.
3. For each asset, propose one preventive control and a way to test whether it works.
**Check understanding:**
- Which term describes this idea: protecting digital systems, accounts and information against misuse or disruption?
- Which practical step best demonstrates cybersecurity in this lesson?
**Project action:** List three digital assets and identify what must be protected
