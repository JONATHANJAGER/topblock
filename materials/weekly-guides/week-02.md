# Course 1 | Week 2: Hacker types, scope and professional ethics

**Company:** Fictional Apex Global Logistics

**Milestone:** Draft a fictional engagement authorization and scope of work

### Session 1: Ethical hacking
**Definition:** Security testing performed with permission and documented boundaries
**Everyday example:** A certified mechanic inspects a truck only with the owner's approval
**Business scenario:** AGL has asked a consultant to perform a review of its own isolated training environment.
**Guided actions:**
1. Create an engagement note: owner, tester, authorized targets, permitted actions, time window and stop condition.
2. Mark every other device, account and outside service out of scope.
3. Practice deciding whether a hypothetical request is within scope; do not scan a real address.
**Check understanding:**
- Which term describes this idea: security testing performed with permission and documented boundaries?
- Which practical step best demonstrates ethical hacking in this lesson?
**Project action:** Write the permission statement for an isolated practice lab
